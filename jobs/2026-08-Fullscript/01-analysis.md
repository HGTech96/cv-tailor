## Seniority & scope signals
- Title: Senior Backend Engineer, on an "infrastructure team" (platform/tooling, not a product feature team)
- Scope is horizontal/cross-team: "multiplier," "unblocking other teams," "supporting product teams," rather than owning a single product surface
- Individual-contributor role but with technical leadership expectations: solution design, release strategy, mentorship, driving foundational upgrades — no explicit people-management language
- Works inside a single large monorepo/monolith rather than a microservices architecture
- Expects independent ownership of ambiguous, cross-cutting technical initiatives (security, upgrades, CI, tooling) rather than narrowly scoped feature tickets

## What they're really hiring for
Fullscript needs a senior engineer to serve as a technical force-multiplier on their core Rails monolith — someone who can independently drive foundational upgrades, security remediation, CI/tooling improvements, and engineering-standards work that unblocks other product teams rather than shipping product features themselves. The repeated emphasis on "long-term health," "maintainability," "pragmatic tradeoffs," and "developer experience" signals a codebase carrying technical debt and friction that slows product teams down, and they want someone who treats internal developer experience and platform reliability as the primary deliverable — proven through cross-team design docs, mentorship, and quiet infrastructure work rather than headline features.
